Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Plain English: We collect what you send us and the basics any website logs. We use it to answer you and run the business. We don't sell it. Ever.

lichtwerk is the trade name of Lichtwerk IT LLC, a Maryland company based in St. Mary's County ("we," "us"). This policy covers our website and how we handle information about prospects and client contacts. Client system data we manage is covered by our client contracts and any Business Associate Agreement, not by this policy.

What we collect

  • What you give us: name, business name, email, phone, and anything you write in a form or email.
  • Automatically: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and similar logs, plus cookies needed to run the site. Our website runs on Squarespace, which uses its own cookies and built-in analytics to count visits and show us how the site is used. We don't use that data for advertising, and we don't sell it.
  • From clients: billing contact details, processed by our payment providers (Square/Squarespace). We do not store full card numbers.

Please don't send health, patient, or other sensitive information through our website forms.

How we use it

To respond to you, schedule assessments, provide and bill for services, keep our systems secure, meet legal obligations, and send occasional service emails. Marketing emails always include an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

Only service providers who help us operate (hosting, email, payments, scheduling, and our IT management and security tools), under confidentiality obligations; authorities when the law requires it; or a successor if the business is sold. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and we protect it with reasonable safeguards, including MFA on our accounts.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information, or opt out of marketing, by emailing info@lichtwerkit.com with "Privacy" in the subject line. We'll respond within 45 days. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Children

Our site is for businesses and isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes

We'll post updates here with a new date.
