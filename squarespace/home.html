FAQ
Do you certify HIPAA or CMMC?

No. We help you get ready, with the controls, documentation and guidance. Certification comes from an authorized assessor, never from your IT company.

We only have three people. Is this for us?

Yes. The minimum is $1,000 a month, so a three-person office pays $1,030 with tax. We'd rather tell you that now than on a call.

Do you come on site?

Yes, across St. Mary's, Calvert and Charles counties, whenever a problem needs hands on the hardware.

What if it's 2 a.m.?

Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8 to 6 Eastern. Threat monitoring runs 24/7, and critical security issues get a response within an hour, any time.

Can we leave?

After month 6, yes, with 30 days' notice and no termination fee, plus any remaining committed license terms. We return your data and hand back every admin login.

What about software licenses?

Microsoft 365 and other licenses are billed at our cost, on their own line. No markup.
